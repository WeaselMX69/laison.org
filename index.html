Banking Cybersecurity

Community banks and credit unions rely on legacy systems that must remain secure while integrating with modern infrastructure. Laison specializes in bridging legacy financial software, modern cybersecurity frameworks, and AI-assisted monitoring.

  • Community bank cybersecurity consulting
  • COBOL system modernization
  • Secure infrastructure deployment
  • AI-assisted fraud monitoring
  • Regulatory compliance alignment

Legacy Core Banking Systems

Many financial institutions still rely on legacy core banking platforms written in COBOL and other long-standing financial languages. These systems power deposits, lending, transaction processing, and interbank settlement across the financial sector.

Laison provides consulting and development support for institutions that must maintain legacy reliability while integrating modern security, automation, and AI monitoring.

  • COBOL system analysis and maintenance
  • Legacy banking platform integration
  • Secure modernization planning
  • Fraud detection and AI monitoring layers
  • Infrastructure hardening for legacy environments

Experience working with legacy financial software allows institutions to extend the lifespan of core banking systems without risking operational disruption.
