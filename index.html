Penetration Testing & Security Consulting

All penetration testing is conducted under written authorization, defined scope, and formal engagement agreements.

  • Network, web application, and API penetration testing
  • Wireless and infrastructure security testing
  • Clear remediation guidance and executive reporting
  • Engagements suitable for municipalities and regulated entities

Parental Monitoring & Child Protection

LAiSON.ORG provides monitoring solutions designed to assist parents and legal guardians in protecting minors from online grooming, exploitation, and predatory behavior.

Services are provided solely with documented parental or legal guardian consent and are limited to the lawful supervision of minors under their care.
