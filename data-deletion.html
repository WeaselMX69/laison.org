Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: March 28, 2026

LAiSON.ORG provides users with the ability to request deletion of their data at any time. This page explains how to request data deletion for any information associated with our services, including integrations with platforms such as Facebook (Meta).

1. How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, please send an email to:

support@laison.org

Include the following information in your request:

  • Your full name
  • Email address associated with your account or interaction
  • Description of the data you want deleted
  • Reference to the platform used (e.g., Facebook, website form, etc.)

2. Identity Verification

To protect user privacy and prevent unauthorized requests, we may require verification of your identity before processing deletion.

3. Processing Time

Data deletion requests are processed within 7–30 days, depending on the complexity of the request.

4. What Gets Deleted

  • Personal contact information
  • Submitted form data
  • Associated usage data linked to your identity

Certain data may be retained if required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Financial record-keeping obligations

5. Third-Party Platforms

If you interacted with LAiSON through a third-party platform (such as Facebook), you may also request deletion directly through that platform.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this process, contact:

support@laison.org
