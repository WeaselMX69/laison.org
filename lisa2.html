To: Barnstable County District Attorney’s Office

Re: Request for Review of Potential Criminal Fraud

I, Lisa McAdams, respectfully request that your office review the following matter for potential criminal fraud.

On or about September 2026, I provided John S. Stockton with a cashier’s check in the amount of $10,000 for the stated purpose of purchasing a vehicle. The check included the memo “truck loan,” and Mr. Stockton initialed next to that memo at the time of transfer, acknowledging that the funds were a loan. This transaction occurred at a bank and was captured on camera.

Mr. Stockton represented that he would repay the loan. These representations are further supported by text message communications between the parties.

Despite these representations, Mr. Stockton has failed to repay the loan. Based on the totality of the circumstances, including the representations made at the time of the transaction, the documented acknowledgment of the loan, and the subsequent failure to repay, I believe Mr. Stockton obtained these funds under false pretenses and did not intend to repay the loan at the time he accepted it.

I have reason to believe that this conduct may not be isolated and may reflect a broader pattern of similar behavior involving other individuals.

I have retained supporting documentation, including a copy of the cashier’s check with memo and initials, and relevant text message communications.

I respectfully request that your office review this matter to determine whether criminal charges are appropriate.

Sincerely,

Lisa McAdams
