Statement

On July 18th, 2026, between the approximate times of 12:16:09 EST and 14:37:14 EST, I, Kevin Goodwin of 24 Church St., Ellsworth, Maine, walked from a friend's house located off Quinaquisset Avenue in Mashpee, Massachusetts, to the Starbucks located at the corner of North Street and Market Street at Mashpee Commons.

During my walk, while traveling southwest along an old paved road through the wooded area near the Mashpee River Reservation, at approximate GPS coordinates:

41.620389, -70.482977

I observed a white male with short cropped hair, approximately 5'8" in height, with a medium build.

I later came to believe this individual may have been Darren Devine after seeing photographs associated with a WVCB news media story regarding criminal investigations and allegations.

At the time I observed him, the individual was approximately 150 feet further southwest along the path, which followed alongside Falmouth Road / Route 28. He was wearing a white t-shirt and appeared to be attempting to retrieve or move something from the brush using a stick approximately four feet long held in his right hand.

My initial interpretation was that he may have been walking with or attempting to assist a dog that was outside of my view.

As I continued down the trail, the individual noticed me. At that point, he discarded the stick to the side and began walking toward me.

When we approached each other, he told me to be careful because there was an injured "great heron" bird further up the trail that appeared to be injured. He indicated something to the effect that he was trying to help it. His behavior appeared nervous, and he seemed to be in a hurry to leave the area.

Observation of the Bird

I continued further down the trail to the area where I had observed the individual standing with the stick. I observed a large heron off the path that appeared to be in distress.

The bird appeared to have visible injuries around both shoulders/wings and appeared to have heavily ruffled neck feathers.

I remained in the area for several minutes. The bird eventually moved into very dense brush between the trail and Falmouth Road. There was little I could do at that time, so I continued on my way.

Subsequent Information

Later that evening, I told my friend about what had occurred but did not think much more about it at the time.

The following day, my friend sent me several links to media reports involving allegations of animal cruelty connected to Darren Devine.

After reviewing those reports and photographs, I believe the individual I encountered may have been Darren Devine. Looking back on the encounter, I believe I may have interrupted him while he was interacting with or harming the bird.
