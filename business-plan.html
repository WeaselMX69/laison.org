LAiSON Strategic Business Plan

Executive Summary

LAiSON is envisioned as a communications, software, artificial intelligence, telecommunications, data interoperability, and public infrastructure company.

The company seeks to build products and services that improve communication between individuals, businesses, governments, healthcare providers, emergency services, financial institutions, telecommunications carriers, and connected devices.

Twenty-Year Objective

Build a trusted communications and interoperability platform capable of serving individuals, businesses, governments, animals, devices, and critical infrastructure through a unified ecosystem.

The objective is not merely to create another software company.

The objective is to build infrastructure that improves communication, efficiency, safety, and quality of life.
