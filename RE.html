1. PURPOSE OF AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to establish an ongoing real estate referral relationship whereby the Referring Party may provide prospective clients, customers, leads, introductions, and business opportunities to the Receiving Broker.

This Agreement applies to all real estate leads originating from the Referring Party, including but not limited to leads transmitted through:

creator@laison.org

or any other written, electronic, digital, telephone, web-based, API, social media, or future communication method controlled by the Referring Party or LAiSON.

2. DEFINITION OF LAiSON REFERRAL

A "LAiSON Referral" means any prospective buyer, seller, landlord, tenant, investor, household, or other real estate client whose information, introduction, inquiry, or business opportunity originates directly or indirectly from Kevin Goodwin or LAiSON.

Each referral may be identified by a unique referral identifier, electronic record, email transmission, or other documentation method.

3. REFERRAL COMPENSATION

For every completed real estate transaction resulting from a LAiSON Referral, the Receiving Broker agrees to pay:

Twenty-Five Percent (25%) of Gross Commission Income

actually received by the Receiving Broker or Brokerage.

Gross Commission Income means total commission compensation received before deductions including brokerage splits, expenses, fees, marketing costs, taxes, or overhead.

4. PAYMENT TERMS

The referral fee shall become due and payable after:

  1. The transaction has successfully closed;
  2. The Receiving Broker or Brokerage has received commission proceeds;
  3. Any required brokerage accounting procedures have been completed.

Payment shall be made by wire transfer according to payment instructions provided by the Referring Party.

5. NON-CIRCUMVENTION AND REFERRAL PROTECTION

The Receiving Broker agrees that it shall not directly or indirectly avoid, bypass, or circumvent the Referring Party's referral rights.

Circumvention includes, but is not limited to:

  • Failing to identify a client as a LAiSON Referral;
  • Transferring the client to another agent or brokerage to avoid payment;
  • Restructuring compensation arrangements to avoid referral obligations;
  • Using an affiliated person, company, spouse, partner, or third party to avoid payment.

The referral compensation obligation survives any reassignment, transfer, continuation, or future transaction involving the referred client.

6. REFERRAL PROTECTION PERIOD

The referral fee obligation applies to any transaction involving a LAiSON Referral occurring during the term of this Agreement and for:

Twenty-Four (24) Months

following the initial referral date.

7. LICENSING COMPLIANCE

The Parties acknowledge that Massachusetts real estate licensing laws regulate the payment of real estate-related compensation.

The Parties intend that all compensation under this Agreement shall be paid in a manner consistent with applicable Massachusetts law, including any requirement that referral compensation be paid through or approved by a licensed Massachusetts broker.

The Referring Party represents that they hold an inactive Massachusetts Real Estate Salesperson License in good standing and that this Agreement is intended solely for lawful referral compensation arrangements.

8. NO AGENCY RELATIONSHIP

Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency relationship between the Parties.

The Receiving Broker retains sole responsibility for licensed real estate activities, client representation, disclosures, negotiations, and compliance obligations.

9. RECORDS AND VERIFICATION

Upon reasonable request, the Receiving Broker shall provide documentation sufficient to verify:

  • Closing date;
  • Commission received;
  • Calculation of referral compensation owed.

10. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement shall remain effective until terminated by either Party through written notice.

Termination shall not affect referral fees earned or obligations relating to LAiSON Referrals submitted before termination.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be interpreted under the applicable laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and, where appropriate, the State of Maine.
